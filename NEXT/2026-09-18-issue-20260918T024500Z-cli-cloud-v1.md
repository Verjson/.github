title: Upgrade the protected deployment CLI to cli-cloud v1

Upgrade the immutable container-deployment contract to `@verjson/cli-cloud@1.0.0`,
including its verified lock graph and the exact behavioral contract assertions.

The major bump was exercised against the installed package, not a transcribed
interface: `verjson-cloud` remains the published `bin`, `runner update <lane>` still
accepts `--cloud`, `--project`, `--runner-mode`, `--release-manifest`,
`--release-variant`, and `--only`, and `runner-host-evidence` still accepts
`--manifest-identity`, `--fleet`, and `--rollback-receipt`'s companion flags, so
`scripts/container_deployment_controller.py` needs no adaptation. The package now
declares `engines.node >= 24.0.0`, which `.github/workflows/container-deployment.yml`
already satisfies at its pinned `24.19.0`.

`docs/v1-readiness/README.md` carried a hand-transcribed copy of this pin that had
already drifted to `0.28.1`; it is corrected in the same commit, since a documented
mirror of an exact pin cannot be caught by the compiler or the contract tests.
