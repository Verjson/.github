title: Remove the privileged route's organization-variable PAT dependency
---

Pass the privileged runner lane through the trusted generated caller and validate the exact self-hosted general selector before scheduling terminal merge, eliminating an unnecessary organization-variable API credential.

The terminal merge remains the only job receiving `ORG_ADMIN_TOKEN`; exact-head, arm-receipt, authorization-App, and maintainer-permission checks are unchanged. See ADR 0089.
